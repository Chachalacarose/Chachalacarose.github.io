About Me

I currently work in the fast-food industry, where I handle food prep for daily needs. With this responsibility comes the challenge of knowing how much food to prepare. There's always a risk of under- or over-prepping! To make things easier, I created a prep list that was easier to follow, helping control waste and keep track of prep times. This is just one of the many reasons I decided to start this new chapter of my life with Tech-Moms. I want to take my ideas to the next level and bring them to life through technology!

Most of my time is spent learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, but when I'm feeling burned out, I like to switch things up. I'll do my nails, enjoy a nature walk with my family, fire up the barbecue, read a good book, or bake a cake.

I have a big family: five kids, four grandkids, three furry grandkitties, and my best friend Bonnie, a bunny. Life is definitely never boring around here!
